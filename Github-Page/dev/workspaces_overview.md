# Local research workspace

`private workspace/` is repository maintainer's private research workspace and is ignored by Git. He works on research notes, experiments, and drafts there; they are not shared evidence until reviewed and published in a tracked deliverable.

repository maintainer is the sole current maintainer and publisher of the repository. `Final-Report/` and `Github-Page/` contain the group's capstone progress reports and research outcomes that he consolidates and publishes. Member names record project activity; they do not indicate current repository access or contributions. Published research results must include their sources, provenance, validation, and limitations.

The academic capstone roster and historical contribution records remain in the official reports. Historical Git commit records are also preserved.
