This folder contains 38 distinct open-access PDF files and their metadata. Use the [references log](references_log.md) for indexed sources and their relevance to the project. Some out-of-scope studies remain linked to their publisher or preprint pages for historical context without a local PDF copy. The references log also links ISO 5807:1985 for the ingress diagram's flowchart notation. Its copyrighted preview is kept in the ignored personal workspace, while the **symbol audit** records the exact clauses used and what the preview cannot verify.

A cited paper supports only the claims established by that source. Local experiment results belong in separate, provenance-backed tracked reports when they are approved for publication. The Review 1 ingress architecture is a project proposal.
