# Paper provenance

- [`He_2023_DeBERTaV3_Disentangled_Attention_ICLR.pdf`](He_2023_DeBERTaV3_Disentangled_Attention_ICLR.pdf) is the DeBERTa-v3 architecture paper. It does not establish the accuracy of a prompt-injection checkpoint.
- [`PIGuard_2025_Prompt_Injection_Guardrail_ACL.pdf`](PIGuard_2025_Prompt_Injection_Guardrail_ACL.pdf) is the paper associated with the public PIGuard checkpoint.

ProtectAI and the other public checkpoints are sourced by their Hugging Face model cards in [`upstream/checkpoints/`](../upstream/checkpoints/). Do not assign the DeBERTa-v3 architecture paper as an experimental paper for checkpoints that do not identify it as such.
