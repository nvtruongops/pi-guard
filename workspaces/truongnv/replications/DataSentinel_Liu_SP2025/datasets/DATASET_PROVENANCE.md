# Dataset provenance — DataSentinel

No local evaluation rows are retained in this package. The former `datasentinel_eval_benchmark.json` (20 rows) was project-authored and lacked verified row-level correspondence to the upstream release; it and its dependent results were withdrawn on 2026-09-29.

Upstream reference: https://github.com/liu00222/Open-Prompt-Injection. The existence of upstream source code does not establish that the withdrawn local rows were published by the authors. The former runner was a local regex/canary heuristic, not a reproduction of the paper’s minimax detector.
