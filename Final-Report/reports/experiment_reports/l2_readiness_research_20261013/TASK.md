## Scope Boundary Declaration

This task continues the user's attached L2 research goal and their instruction to make L2 ready, rather than stopping at an assessment of missing evidence. The completed V9 health check remains an audit record, not the execution boundary for this research.

IN-SCOPE: prepare an immutable V10 candidate successor from authorized V9 public candidates; recover original split/file and contributor grouping evidence; identify cross-family duplicate constraints; provide a small source-blind curator packet and a runnable integrity/decision intake check; research additional public source documentation; record every actual run and remaining human gate.

OUT-OF-SCOPE: assigning canonical labels, approving rights/privacy on behalf of a reviewer, inventing acceptance limits, accessing historical locked validation/test/source-evaluation payloads, fitting before data gates pass, L3, commit and publication.

## Implementation choice

Use a V10 provenance overlay with references to the pinned V9 queues and source snapshots. Copy only a small deterministic review packet of existing records. This addresses missing upstream split/group evidence without duplicating the full corpus or creating new prompt text. A full corpus copy adds no evidence; another large source ingest would increase review work before resolving existing source coverage. Public prospects remain metadata-only where public-release scope or context is unresolved.
