R05 is the best current source-diverse comparator; later source additions remain provisional and have not passed the official, still-unspecified acceptance gate.

## Current state and next useful work

The project result remains NOT_READY and the official status remains GATE_UNSPECIFIED. R05 is the best prior comparator; R06, R27, R28, and R29 add one provisional JB family each but regress aggregate validation and/or pooled OOF. R27's heldout JBB-family recall is 0.2024; R28's pooled JB recall is 0.1072; R29's pooled JB F1 is 0.5146, below R05's 0.5552. No local diagnostic limit or source-card label substitutes for an official acceptance rubric or human-gold adjudication.

The evidence points to source-family coverage and record-level rights/lineage as the next high-value L2 direction. R23 shows that excluding a semantically unsupported JB mapping can improve validation and OOF; R24 and R27–R29 show that adding one provisional JB family at a time can lower aggregate quality. R28's algorithmic benchmark, R29's NeurAlchemy component, and R30–R31's overlapping, private, or generated sources do not establish independent human-source coverage. Round19 CLINC and R22 Banking77 remain provisional benign references with incomplete privacy review. R25–R31 did not find an eligible independent single-turn JB train source. Continue first-party source qualification; do not repeat parameter sweeps or reuse rejected additions on R05/R06/R27/R28/R29.

PI-Guard's final-test payload, L3, cascade, smoke, commit, or publication was not used for rounds 21–31. R28 and R30 external public-page example previews were quarantined and unused; earlier previews were disclosed in their source reports.
