This folder contains 38 distinct open-access PDF files and their metadata. Use the [references log](REFERENCES_LOG.md) for indexed sources and their relevance to the project. Some out-of-scope studies remain linked to their publisher or preprint pages for historical context without a local PDF copy. The local PIGuard file is the published ACL 2025 version; its earlier *InjecGuard* preprint is linked, not duplicated.

The references log also links ISO 5807:1985 for the ingress diagram's flowchart notation. Its copyrighted preview is kept in the ignored personal workspace, while the [symbol audit](../reports/ISO_5807_1985_SYMBOL_AUDIT.md) records the exact clauses used and what the preview cannot verify.

A cited paper supports only the claims established by that source. Local experiment results belong in separate, provenance-backed tracked reports when they are approved for publication. The Review 1 ingress architecture is a project proposal.
